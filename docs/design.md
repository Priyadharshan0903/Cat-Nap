# Design decisions

Direction: Pastel consumer, seed 189953084, from the frontend design skill. Adapted to a puzzle interface rather than a finance dashboard. No direction redraws.

The screen's question is “Where does the next cat belong?” The colored territory board is the largest element. The companion window and original cat drawings give the product its face. Warm paper, forest ink, bundled Manrope, flat surfaces, and rounded controls support the quiet experience. Color distinguishes regions and cats rather than decorating statistics. The desktop grid is 220 + 480 + 220 + 2×48 = 1016px, inside 18px side padding; the responsive layout gives the board priority and folds navigation to the bottom on iPhone.

The palette budget is deliberately relaxed for puzzle data: distinct region colors are part of the rules. Optional region numbers provide another way to identify regions. Stage numbers and chapter numbers are real level identifiers. “Unlock” describes the actual cat progression mechanic, not marketing copy. The companion label supplies context for the illustrated cat. These account for the source style inspector's warnings. Shadows belong only to the floating hint panel, toast, and modal dialog, where the style permits them.

Motion is feedback: spring placement, X marks, hint highlighting, and a short completion celebration. There is no waiting for an animation to finish before the next move. System reduced-motion preferences and the in-game gentler-motion setting are honored. Audio, haptics, and music are absent.

The level selector answers “What can I play next?” All levels are available. The cat collection answers “Who keeps me company?” Settings answers “How do I make this comfortable and keep my progress?” All figures come from actual local progress; there are no sample achievements or fabricated metrics.

Connections and stage numbers are actual map data; the current-position outline is a selected-state indicator. The map adapts the supplied screenshot’s wayfinding concept while retaining Catnap’s established visual identity. Reward pockets show real saved balances; all refills stay free.

The v1.2 changes retain Pastel consumer (seed 189953084), the layout, Manrope, and the original cat/window artwork; no direction redraw was needed. A scrim around a teaching deduction brings the actual rows, columns, and colors into focus; the optional action marks exclusions. Correctly completed regions receive a short wash and a small check; the cat smiles. This positive confirmation applies only to correct placements, so an incorrect but conflict-free cat does not appear completed. All new movement has a reduced-motion fallback.

Source lint: 0 failures, 17 existing warnings, explained above. The v1.2 real-browser inspector was attempted normally and outside the sandbox; Chrome exits before rendering in this environment. No new screenshots or pixel-based critique scores are claimed. Interaction, save-transfer, and offline harness checks cover behavior; touch experience is being evaluated by the user.

Hint reasons now appear in a sticky floating panel before the board, with a short spring entrance, stronger readable copy, and an Apply action beside Dismiss. Opening a hint scrolls its natural anchor into view and focuses the explanation; Escape dismisses it. The panel has a bounded scrolling height on short screens. The board moves smoothly as the panel appears or leaves, and applying an exclusion staggers the new X marks by a capped 180 ms. Focus outlines pulse twice rather than indefinitely. System and in-app reduced-motion settings continue to suppress movement.

After the hint-panel update, source lint reports 0 failures and 18 warnings. The added warning is the shadow on the sticky floating explanation, permitted for an overlay. The source direction and all prior warning explanations remain the same; no new image assets or sample data were added.

Hint targets use a 1px outline with a 6px corner radius, inset 1px from cell edges. Hints leave corner numbers off unless the player explicitly enables the accessibility preference. Explanations use stable color names (Gold, Mint, Sky blue, Peach, Sage, Rose, Teal, Sand, Slate) with small matching swatches, so numbered colors are no longer needed.

The hint scrim extends over each dimmed cell’s top and left borders using their actual widths. This prevents undimmed border paint from leaving pale seams through the darker area; normal grid boundaries stay intact outside hint mode. The highlight pulse now stays at 65% opacity or above for steadier thin outlines.
